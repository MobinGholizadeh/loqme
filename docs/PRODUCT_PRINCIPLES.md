# Permanent Product Principles — Loqme (لقمه)

> **Document Status**: Immutable Product Constitution  
> Every feature, copy snippet, architectural choice, and algorithmic rule must comply with these 15 principles.

---

### 1. Loqme Sells Value, Not Waste
Loqme is an engine of culinary delight and economic value. We connect customers to delicious, fresh, perfectly edible food that happens to be surplus. We never frame offerings as scraps, leftovers, or discards. Customers buy because the product is genuinely desirable, not out of pity.

### 2. Trust Beats Short-Term Conversion
If a user purchases a Surprise Bag and experiences substandard items, misleading quantities, or unhygienic presentation, they will abandon the platform permanently. We will aggressively delist unreliable merchants or penalize misleading listings, even if doing so reduces short-term gross merchandise value (GMV).

### 3. Discovery is the Primary Emotional Driver
The core emotional reward of using Loqme is the joy of discovery—stumbling upon an artisanal bakery in your neighborhood, trying an exotic pastry, or unlocking a delightful surprise package at an unbeatable price. The interface must celebrate visual freshness, serendipity, and neighborhood exploration.

### 4. Saving Money is the Primary Functional Benefit
While the emotional hook is discovery, the utilitarian foundation is tangible financial savings. In an inflationary economic climate, consumers need immediate, verifiable value. Every listing must clearly display honest, genuine savings in Tomans without hidden fees.

### 5. Sustainability is Positive Secondary Reinforcement
Preventing food waste is an extraordinary, inspiring outcome of our business, but it must never be used as guilt-inducing marketing. Eco-impact metrics (e.g. estimated CO₂ or food saved) should be celebrated as empowering badges of honor, never as guilt trips.

### 6. Never Use Deceptive Scarcity
Inventory counters must reflect literal physical stock in the database. Never display fabricated alerts such as "فقط ۲ نفر دیگر در حال مشاهده این آیتم هستند" (Only 2 other people are viewing this) or fake countdown clocks that reset upon page refresh. Scarcity on Loqme is authentic because daily surplus is naturally finite.

### 7. Never Manipulate Reference Prices
Reference prices (original retail price / ارزش واقعی) must correspond to the merchant's standard menu or in-store shelf price. Inflating the original price to make a discount look artificially higher is severe fraud and results in immediate merchant suspension.

### 8. Transparency Beats Conversion When the Two Conflict
If a Surprise Bag cannot accommodate gluten-free diets, if dairy is present, or if pickup requires bringing your own container, this information must be prominent before checkout. We refuse to obscure critical constraints just to boost conversion rates.

### 9. Don't Intentionally Create Surplus
Loqme exists to optimize legitimate operational variance, not to incentivize overproduction. If analytics indicate that a merchant is routinely cooking extra batches solely to sell on Loqme at discounted margins, the platform will intervene.

### 10. Pickup-First MVP
Physical in-person pickup is fundamental to Loqme's MVP. It eliminates expensive logistics, guarantees food freshness at handoff, and drives valuable neighborhood foot traffic straight into local brick-and-mortar storefronts.

### 11. Simple UX Beats Feature Quantity
A streamlined, lightning-fast 3-tap checkout loop is infinitely superior to an overloaded interface. We prioritize low latency, intuitive Persian RTL ergonomics, clear typography, and effortless QR presentation over superfluous bells and whistles.

### 12. Marketplace Density Matters
A marketplace with 500 merchants scattered across an entire province is useless to a consumer who walks 10 minutes to dinner. We prioritize hyper-local neighborhood density (Tehran districts first: Sa'adat Abad, Shahrak-e Gharb, Enghelab, Tajrish) before geographic expansion.

### 13. Merchant and Customer Value Must Both Exist
A two-sided marketplace cannot survive if either side feels exploited. Customers must receive genuine discounts; merchants must recover food preparation costs and earn positive margin. We do not extract punishing commissions that bankrupt small neighborhood artisans.

### 14. Safety is Non-Negotiable
Food safety is an absolute prerequisite. Products past official expiration dates, unsealed items with broken hygiene seals, or improperly chilled temperature-sensitive foods are strictly barred from the platform. When in doubt, the item must not be listed.

### 15. Legal Uncertainty Must Never Be Turned into an Unverified Assumption
We operate within Iranian legal frameworks (E-Commerce Law, Ministry of Health regulations, and Guild licensing). Where regulations are ambiguous or evolving, we tag them explicitly as assumptions in [LEGAL_ASSUMPTIONS.md](file:///C:/Users/etraab/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/loqme/docs/LEGAL_ASSUMPTIONS.md) and obtain professional counsel rather than guessing.
