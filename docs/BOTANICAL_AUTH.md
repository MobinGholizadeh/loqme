## Loading and failure behavior

The loader is server-rendered. The experience remains invisible, inert and hidden from assistive technology until the selected image has loaded and `decode()` resolves. Cached success and failure before hydration are handled. After 12 seconds, the loader offers retry and an explicit image-free continuation; it does not automatically reveal an incomplete scene. Failed downloads offer the same choices immediately. Choosing to continue uses a composed green fallback, and later image completion does not override that choice. Keyboard focus moves to the form after a recovery action. Reduced motion disables the breathing animation.

## Artwork and resolution

Artwork was created using the built-in image-generation tool. The generator returned **1672×941 landscape and 941×1672 portrait** originals despite 4K prompts. Delivery exports include **3840×2160** and **2160×3840**, upscaled with Lanczos resampling. These are 4K-sized exports, **not native 4K generated detail**.

Project assets live in `apps/web/public/images/auth/`. `scripts/prepare-auth-images.cjs` exports WebP variants from the two source PNGs. The browser selects an appropriate variant through `<picture>` / `srcset`, with eager high-priority loading and no runtime image-optimizer cold start. Mobile widths are 480 (30 KiB), 960 (77 KiB) and 2160 (208 KiB); desktop widths are 1280 (139 KiB), 1920 (228 KiB), 2560 (313 KiB) and 3840 (491 KiB). Size selection depends on viewport and device pixel ratio. No third-party image host is required.
